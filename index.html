Project Description: EduFocus is a next-generation virtual study room platform designed to eliminate student isolation and boost productivity. It enables learners worldwide to join live, moderated focus rooms, track milestones, share resources, and study alongside peers seamlessly.

Vision: To cultivate a global, accessible, and distraction-free digital ecosystem where every student feels motivated to unlock their full academic potential.
